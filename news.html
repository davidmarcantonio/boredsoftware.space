  1. Nov 14, 2026Bored Room

    Bored Room 1.1 is on the App Store

    Apple approved it, so the update from yesterday's post is out. Free for anyone who bought 1.0.

    • Set up with iPhone. On the iPad, tap "Set up with iPhone" and scan the code with Bored Room on your phone. Connect Google Calendar and answer the questions there. The iPad keeps the sign-in, the phone keeps nothing, and the two talk directly (guest Wi-Fi is fine). Later, Settings › Room › "Change from iPhone" moves an iPad to another room. Whether you need the iPhone copy at all: the decision chart. Short version: it's worth it from about six iPads without an MDM.
    • One MDM config for every iPad. calendarIds lists your rooms once; each iPad's picker only shows those. It's in the Room key table now.
    • Plainer wording. "Connect Google Calendar" replaces "Sign in with Google", because Bored Room has no accounts of its own.

    The setup guides on boredroom.space cover all of it. Still not in this one: the light-up case support. It comes in a later update, once it's been tested on an actual case.

  2. Nov 13, 2026Bored Room

    Bored Room 1.1 is in App Review

    Two things in this one:

    • Set up with iPhone. The iPad shows a code, your iPhone scans it, you sign in with Google and pick the room on the phone, and the setup hands over on your local network. No typing on a wall-mounted iPad, and still no server of ours in the middle. It's for offices with a handful of iPads and no MDM; the iPhone needs its own copy of Bored Room.
    • One MDM config for every iPad. The new calendarIds key lists your rooms once; each iPad's picker only shows those rooms. No per-room profiles. The key goes into the Room support page when 1.1 ships.

    Apple decides when it's out. We'll post here when it is.

  6. Oct 29, 2026Bored Scanner

    Meet Bored Scanner

    The second app came from a pallet of laptops. Photograph an asset label and Scanner keeps the serial and MAC addresses and skips the fine print. Capture to a CSV with no setup at all, or connect Snipe-IT to assign, look up and retire from your iPhone or iPad. Same deal: 99¢, no backend.

    It's in testing now. More on boredscanner.space, and the backstory is on David's blog: I Got Bored, So I Made Some Apps.

  7. Oct 18, 2026Bored Room

    Google verified Bored Room's sign-in

    Google finished its review of how Bored Room signs in. That means no "unverified app" warning when you connect a calendar. The app asks for one permission, calendar events (calendar.events), so it can show the room's day and book walk-ins. The details are in Bored Room's privacy page.
